# Code of Conduct

AppFiler is a small project built by people who care about privacy and free software. This page sets out how we treat each other, so that everyone who wants to take part feels able to.

## What's expected

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, never attack people. Assume the person on the other side means well, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- **Be patient.** Many contributors are volunteers working in their spare time and in their second or third language. A slow or short reply is rarely meant as rudeness.
- **Be constructive.** If you criticise something, say what would make it better. In reviews, explain the reason behind a request so the other person can learn from it.
- **Welcome newcomers.** Everyone started somewhere. Answer basic questions kindly, or point to where the answer is.
- **Respect privacy.** This project exists to protect people's privacy, and that applies to the people in it. Don't share anyone's personal information without their permission.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or intimidation, in public or in private messages
- Remarks that demean people for who they are, including their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability or level of experience
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a home address or an email address they haven't made public
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or trolling
- Encouraging or supporting any of the above

## Where it applies

This code applies everywhere the project lives: the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and any forum thread or chat where someone is acting on the project's behalf.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are read in confidence, and the person reporting won't be identified without their agreement.

## What happens next

The maintainer will look into every report and respond fairly. Depending on how serious it is, the response can range from a private word, to a public warning, to removing comments, to a temporary or permanent ban from the project. Anyone may appeal a decision by writing to the maintainer.
